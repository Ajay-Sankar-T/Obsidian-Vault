###### Description

To extend the knowledge acquired in Thermodynamics by an in-depth study of the second law and exergy analyses. Also, introduce air conditioning and refrigeration systems

###### Course Content

Different energy sources and major processes involving energy utilisation Exergy, maximum or reversible work, exergy balance and Exergy analysis. Thermodynamic cycles: Rankine (basic, superheat, reheat, regenerative), Air standard Brayton cycle (basic, two-stage, intercooling, reheat and regenerative), First and second law analyses Commonly used refrigerants and their properties. Vapor compression refrigeration cycles Vapor refrigeration systems and their analysis. Actual cycles - superheating, sub-cooling and liquid suction heat exchanger – multistage and cascade systems. Introduction to Psychrometric principles. Application of mass and energy balances to air-conditioning systems. Wet- and dry-bulb temperatures. Pschyrometric chart. Air conditioning processes.

###### Description

The students will be introduced to combustion reactions, internal combustion engines and turbomachines

###### Course Content

General overview of the course, connection with Foundations of Thermal Engg -1 course. Importance of combustion process and turbomachines in power plants Combustion reactions – Stoichiometry. First law analysis, Heat calculations, Adiabatic flame temperature Air standard Otto, Diesel and Dual cycles, Evolution of IC engines; Features of IC engines; Nomenclature; Classification; Construction and working of 2S, 4S, petrol and diesel engines; Components of the IC engines; Valve and port timing diagrams; P-v diagrams; Engine test parameters and their calculations, IC Engines combustion and emissions, Alternate fuels (Bio-fuel and Hydrogen) and their prospects in IC engines, Hybrid Electric Vehicles (HEVs) and their prospects. Turbomachine and positive displacement machines. Types of turbomachines. Velocity triangle. Euler equation for turbomachines and degree of reaction. Non-dimensional groups in turbomachines. Compressible flow: compressibility, speed of sound One-dimensional compressible flow in nozzle. Thermodynamics of turbomachines. Efficiencies of turbomachines. Performance Characteristics of Turbomachines.
