# Fluid Mechanics — Introduction and Fundamental Concepts

← up: [[Fluid Mechanics/Fluid Mechanics - MOC|Fluid Mechanics MOC]]

## Main idea

Fluid mechanics studies the behavior of **liquids and gases**, focusing on how they move, what forces act on them, and how properties like pressure, velocity, density, and viscosity describe that motion.

## Why fluid mechanics matters

Fluid flow is present throughout natural and engineering systems: breathing, blood circulation, atmospheric pressure, pipe flow, aircraft, submarines, and space systems — the subject matters both for daily-life intuition and for engineering design.
