# Absolute, Gauge and Vacuum Pressure

← up: [[Fluid Mechanics/Fluid Mechanics - MOC|Fluid Mechanics MOC]] · builds on: [[Fluid Mechanics/02 Fluid Statics/Pressure and the Hydrostatic Equation|Pressure and the Hydrostatic Equation]]

## Definitions

$$\boxed{P_g=P_{abs}-P_{atm}}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \boxed{P_{abs}=P_{atm}+P_g}$$

$$\boxed{P_{vac}=P_{atm}-P_{abs}}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \boxed{P_{abs}=P_{atm}-P_{vac}}$$

Standard atmospheric pressure: $\boxed{P_{atm}\approx101.325\ \text{kPa}}$.

## Memory picture

```text
Absolute
   ↑
   │ Gauge  (positive: above atmospheric)
   ↓
Atmospheric  ← reference (P_gauge = 0 here)
   │
   ↓
Vacuum  (P_abs below atmospheric)
```

Gauge pressure is what most pressure gauges actually read (they're calibrated to zero at atmospheric pressure); absolute pressure is what appears in the ideal-gas law and in every derivation from [[Fluid Mechanics/02 Fluid Statics/Pressure and the Hydrostatic Equation|Pressure and the Hydrostatic Equation]] — mixing the two is one of the most common sources of sign errors in this topic.
