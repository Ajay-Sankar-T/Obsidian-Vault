Practice

Read the problem, open only the hint, try it on paper, then lift the flap for the solution and mark yourself honestly. Problems you solve get a stamp.

How to attack a problem
  1. Say what is asked and what is given.
  2. Choose the principle before calculating.
  3. Solve symbolically, then plug in numbers.
  4. Check units and a limiting case.
