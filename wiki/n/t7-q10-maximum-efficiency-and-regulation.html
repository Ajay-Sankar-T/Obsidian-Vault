T7-Q10 — Regulation from the Maximum-Efficiency Condition

EE1100transformertutorial-solutionefficiencyvoltage-regulation

Hub: Tutorial 7 on Transformer · Previous: T7-Q9 - Efficiency and Regulation from Tests

The maximum efficiency of a 100 kVA single-phase transformer is 98 % and occurs at 80 % of full load at 0.8 pf. The leakage impedance is 5 %. Find the voltage regulation at rated load, 0.8 pf lagging. [3.75 %]

Tests: maximum-efficiency condition, per-unit quantities.

Given

S=100 kVA; ηmax​=0.98 at x=0.8, cosϕ=0.8; ∣ze​∣=0.05 pu. No voltages are given, so work per unit (V2​=1, I2​=1 at rated load).

Principle and why it applies

At maximum efficiency, copper loss equals core loss, at that load. That gives the copper loss, hence the resistance, in pu; the impedance magnitude then gives the reactance.

Solution

  1. Output at maximum efficiency: Po​=0.8×100 kVA×0.8=64 kW.
  2. η=Po​+2Pc​Po​​=0.98 ⇒ 2Pc​=64(0.981​−1)=1.3061 kW, so Pc​=Pcu​(0.8)=653.1 W.
  3. Copper loss at full load: Pcu,FL​=0.82653.1​=1020.4 W, so

re​=1000001020.4​=0.010204 pu (1.02 %).

  1. xe​=ze2​−re2​​=0.052−0.0102042​=0.04895 pu.
  2. Regulation (0.8 lag): VR=re​cosϕ+xe​sinϕ=0.010204(0.8)+0.04895(0.6)=0.00816+0.02937=0.03753.

Result

VR=3.75 %

Check

At full load the losses are 1020+653=1673 W on an output of 80 kW, so ηFL​=80+1.67380​=97.95 %, a little below the 98 % maximum at 80 % load, as it should be.

Trap / alternative

Lecture 39 solves this same problem but takes the whole 5 % as reactance (xe​=0.05), getting VR=0.0102(0.8)+0.05(0.6)=3.82 %. The impedance is the vector sum of re​ and xe​, so the reactance is 0.04895, not 0.05, and the sheet's 3.75 % is the right answer. The error is small only because re​≪xe​.
