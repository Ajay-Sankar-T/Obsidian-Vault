AM2200 Tutorial 5 — Q1: Axially Loaded Member

Question 1 asks us to analyze a stepped bar ABCD, fixed between two rigid supports, with a point load P applied at B. The area is A1​ from A to C, and 2A1​ from C to D. The lengths are AB=L/4, BC=L/4, and CD=L/2.

1. Theory

A. Axial deformation

For a prismatic member subjected to a constant axial force:

δ=AENL​​

where N = internal axial force, L = length, A = cross-sectional area, E = Young's modulus.

Sign convention: Tension (N>0, elongation +), Compression (N<0, shortening −). For several segments:

δtotal​=i∑​Ai​ENi​Li​​​

B. Rigid-support compatibility

Since both A and D are rigid supports:

δD​−δA​=0​⇒∑δi​=0​

This compatibility condition is essential because the reactions are statically indeterminate.

2. Solution

Q1(a) — Reactions RA​ and RD​

Let positive force be to the right. ∑Fx​=0⇒RA​+P+RD​=0(1).

Internal axial forces:

  • Segment AB: NAB​=−RA​
  • Segments BC and CD: NBC​=NCD​=−(RA​+P)

Apply compatibility:

A1​E(−RA​)(L/4)​+A1​E[−(RA​+P)](L/4)​+(2A1​)E[−(RA​+P)](L/2)​=0

Simplifying:

RA​+2(RA​+P)=0⇒3RA​+2P=0⇒RA​=−32P​​

From Eq. (1): RD​=−P−RA​⇒RD​=−3P​​.

Q1(b) — Displacements δB​ and δC​

  • NAB​=32P​ (Tension)
  • NBC​=NCD​=−3P​ (Compression)

Displacement of B:

δB​=A1​ENAB​LAB​​=A1​E(2P/3)(L/4)​=6A1​EPL​​

Displacement of C:

δC​=δAB​+δBC​=6A1​EPL​−A1​E(P/3)(L/4)​=12A1​EPL​​

Q1(c) — Displacement diagram

The displacement is piecewise linear.

  • δA​=0
  • δB​=6A1​EPL​
  • δC​=12A1​EPL​
  • δD​=0

Key Exam Idea

Statically indeterminate axial member ⇒ Combine:

Equilibrium+Compatibility+Axial deformation​
