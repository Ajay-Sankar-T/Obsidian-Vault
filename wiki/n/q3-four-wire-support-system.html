AM2200 Tutorial 5 — Q3: Four-Wire Support System

Q3 asks for the vertical displacement of the 800-lb load supported by four identical 304 stainless-steel wires. Each wire has A=0.05 in2,E=28(103) ksi. Upper wires: 4 ft. Lower wires: 4.5 ft. Rigid members AB and DC are initially horizontal. DC=7 ft,AB=6 ft. Load is 1 ft from A.

1. Theory

Axial deformation: δ=AETL​​. Rigid members: They do not deform; displacement varies linearly along the member.

2. Solution

Forces in lower wires (Rigid member AB)

Taking moments about A: ∑MA​=0⇒TB​(6)−800(1)=0⇒TB​=133.33 lb​. Vertical equilibrium: TA​+TB​=800⇒TA​=666.67 lb​.

Deformation of lower wires (L=54 in,E=28×106 psi)

  • δA,lower​=0.05×28×106666.67×54​=0.02571 in​
  • δB,lower​=0.05×28×106133.33×54​=0.005143 in​

Forces and Deformation of upper wires (L=48 in)

Forces are the same as lower wires: TDE​=666.67 lb,TCF​=133.33 lb.

  • δD,upper​=0.05×28×106666.67×48​=0.022857 in​
  • δC,upper​=0.05×28×106133.33×48​=0.004571 in​

Total Displacement of A and B

  • δA​=δD,upper​+δA,lower​=0.022857+0.025714=0.048571 in​
  • δB​=δC,upper​+δB,lower​=0.004571+0.005143=0.009714 in​

Displacement of the Load (H)

Load is 1/6 of the way from A to B:

δH​=δA​+61​(δB​−δA​)=0.048571+61​(0.009714−0.048571)=0.0421 in downward​
