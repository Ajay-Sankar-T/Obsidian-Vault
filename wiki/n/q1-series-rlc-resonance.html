EE1100 Tutorial 4 — Q1: Series RLC Resonance

A series RLC circuit: R=10Ω,L=0.01H,C=100μF.

1. Theory

  • Resonant Frequency: ω0​=LC​1​
  • Quality Factor: Q=Rω0​L​=R1​CL​​
  • Bandwidth: BW=Qω0​​=LR​
  • Frequency Points: ω1​≈ω0​−2BW​, ω2​≈ω0​+2BW​ (for high Q). Exact: ω1,2​=ω02​+(2BW​)2​∓2BW​.

2. Solution

(a) Resonant Frequency

ω0​=0.01×100×10−6​1​=10−6​1​=1000rad/s​

(b) Quality Factor

Q=101000×0.01​=1​

(c) Bandwidth

BW=Qω0​​=11000​=1000rad/s​

(d) Frequency Points Since Q=1, the approximation ω0​±BW/2 is poor. We use:

ω1,2​=10002+5002​∓500=1118.03∓500

  • ω1​=1118.03−500=618.03rad/s​
  • ω2​=1118.03+500=1618.03rad/s​

(e) Voltage across Capacitor Signal e(t)=1sin(1000t). Since ω=1000=ω0​, the circuit is at resonance. At resonance, Z=R=10Ω. Current Imax​=RVmax​​=101​=0.1A. Voltage across capacitor VC,max​=Imax​×ω0​C1​=0.1×1000×100×10−61​=0.1×10=1V​.
