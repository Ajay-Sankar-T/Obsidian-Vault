EE1100 Tutorial 4 — Q8: FM Receiver BPF Design

Problem: Tune to 100MHz. Attenuate 98MHz and 102MHz by at least 20dB. R=100Ω. Design L and C.

1. Theory

Attenuation in dB: The power ratio is P(ω)/P(ω0​)=∣H(ω)∣2/∣H(ω0​)∣2. 20dB attenuation means ∣H(ω)∣2/∣H(ω0​)∣2≤10−2=0.01. Thus, ∣H(ω)∣/∣H(ω0​)∣≤0.1.

For a series RLC BPF (output across R):
