T3-Q3: RL Series Circuit, Complex Impedance and the Applied Voltage

EE1100tutorial-solutionAC-circuitsimpedanceRL-circuit

Hub: Tutorial 3 - AC Circuit Analysis · Theory: Impedance and Series-Parallel AC Circuits, Phasors and Complex Algebra · Previous: T3-Q2 - RMS of a Staircase Waveform · Next: T3-Q4 - RC Series Voltage Drops

A circuit has R=9 Ω in series with an inductive reactance of 12 Ω. The steady-state current is i(t)=28.3sin377t. (a) Complex impedance? (b) Time expression for the applied voltage? (c) The inductance in henrys? [Sheet: Z=9+j12 Ω; v(t)=424.5sin(377t+53.13∘) V; L=31.83 mH]

Tests: impedance of a series RL, converting a sinusoid to a phasor and back.

Given

R=9 Ω, XL​=12 Ω, i(t)=28.3sin(377t) A, so ω=377 rad/s (f=60 Hz), Im​=28.3 A, current phase 0∘.

Principle and why it applies

In sinusoidal steady state every voltage and current is a sinusoid at the same ω, so each can be represented by a phasor (magnitude and phase) and the circuit obeys Vˉ=IˉZ with a complex Z=R+jX. An inductor's impedance is +jXL​ (voltage leads current by 90∘), so a series RL has Z=R+jXL​ with a positive angle, and the applied voltage leads the current by that angle.

Solution

(a) Z=R+jXL​=(9+j12) Ω=15∠53.13∘ Ω (∣Z∣=81+144​=15, θ=arctan912​).

(b) Using peak phasors: Iˉm​=28.3∠0∘, so

Vˉm​=Iˉm​Z=28.3×15 ∠(0∘+53.13∘)=424.5∠53.13∘ V ⇒ v(t)=424.5sin(377t+53.13∘) V.

(In rms terms: I=20∠0∘ A, V=300∠53.13∘ V.)

(c) XL​=ωL⇒L=37712​=31.83 mH.

Result

Z=9+j12 Ω=15∠53.13∘ Ω;v(t)=424.5sin(377t+53.13∘) V;L=31.83 mH​

Check

Phase: tanθ=XL​/R=1.333⇒53.13∘ (inductive, voltage leads). Real power P=Irms2​R=400×9=3600 W and S=Vrms​Irms​=300×20=6000 VA give pf=0.6 lagging =cos53.13∘ ✓.

Trap / alternative

  • Voltage leads current in an inductor; writing −53.13∘ is the capacitive case.
  • Keep peak and rms straight: the printed 424.5 V is a peak value (=15×28.3).
  • L is XL​/ω with ω=377 rad/s, not XL​/f.

Concepts used

  • Impedance, phasors, inductive reactance (Impedance and Series-Parallel AC Circuits, Phasors and Complex Algebra)
