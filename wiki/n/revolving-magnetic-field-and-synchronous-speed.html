Revolving Magnetic Field and Synchronous Speed

EE1100conceptinduction-machinerotating-fieldthree-phase

Related: 11 Three Phase Induction Machines · Rotating magnetic field (glossary) · 09 Three Phase Circuits and Power Measurement · Induction Motor - Slip, Rotor Circuit and Power Flow · animation: Three-phase revolving field

What it is

Three identical stator windings placed 120∘ apart in space and fed by three currents 120∘ apart in time produce a magnetic field of constant magnitude that rotates at a fixed speed, the synchronous speed ns​. This is why a three-phase induction motor needs no moving contact and is self-starting.

Derivation (Lecture 41)

Currents ia​=Im​sinωt, ib​=Im​sin(ωt−120∘), ic​=Im​sin(ωt+120∘) in windings of Nph​ turns. Each winding produces a sinusoidally space-distributed MMF along its own axis. With α the angle measured from the axis of phase a and Fm​=Nph​Im​:

Fa​=Fm​sinωtcosα,Fb​=Fm​sin(ωt−120∘)cos(α−120∘),Fc​=Fm​sin(ωt+120∘)cos(α+120∘).

Add them. Using 2sinAcosB=sin(A−B)+sin(A+B),

FR​=Fa​+Fb​+Fc​=2Fm​​[3 equal termssin(ωt−α)+sin(ωt−α)+sin(ωt−α)​​+=0sin(ωt+α)+sin(ωt+α−240∘)+sin(ωt+α+240∘)​​]

FR​(α,t)=23​Fm​sin(ωt−α)​

The backward-travelling terms sin(ωt+α+…) are three phasors 120∘ apart and cancel. What is left is a single travelling wave: at ωt=0 the peak is at α=−90∘, at ωt=45∘ it has moved by 45∘, and so on. The pattern rotates at angular speed ω (electrical) with constant peak 23​Fm​.

Synchronous speed

For P poles (pole pairs P/2), one electrical cycle moves the field by one pole pair:

θelec​=2P​θmech​,ωelec​=2P​ωmech​,f=2P​ns​ (ns​ in rev/s),

ns​=P120f​ rpm​(50 Hz: P=2,4,6⇒3000,1500,1000 rpm).

Direction. Interchanging any two phase connections reverses the sequence (b↔c) and the field rotates the other way: the standard way to reverse a three-phase motor.

Single-phase machines. One winding gives a pulsating field, equal to two counter-rotating fields of half amplitude, with no net starting torque. A second winding with a phase shift (25∘–30∘ in a split-phase motor, helped by a capacitor in a capacitor-start motor) makes the field rotate during starting (Induction Machines - Types, Construction and Applications).

Traps
  • ns​=120f/P uses the number of poles, not pole pairs (P=2p).
  • The speed is fixed by the supply frequency only. The load does not change ns​; it changes the rotor speed (slip).
  • The amplitude is 23​Fm​ (one and a half times the single-phase amplitude), not 3Fm​.

Cross-domain links

  • Rotating Vectors - Phasors, Velocity Polygons and Complex Kinematics — a phasor ejωt is a rotating vector; here three alternating quantities, each the projection of a rotating vector, combine into one genuinely rotating field. The cancellation of the backward terms is the sum of the three cube roots of unity being zero.
  • Eigenvalues, Rotation and Diagonalization Everywhere — the three-phase currents are the projections of a vector rotating at ω; the 120∘ symmetry is what makes the resulting rotation uniform.
