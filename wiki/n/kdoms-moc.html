Kinematics and dynamics of machinery (ME 2201). Source text: Theory of Mechanisms and Machines (Ghosh & Mallik) — the PDF is local only (git-ignored). Each bank below is a formula sheet with equation numbers from the book, worked benchmarks, figures in its own assets/ folder, and Python/HTML tools for checking numbers.

Main spine

  1. Velocity and acceleration of a mechanism — find motion of every link, given the input - Analytical method — loop closure, differentiated; linear in the unknowns - Graphical methods (polygons) — velocity/acceleration polygons, images, instantaneous centres
  2. Synthesis — design a mechanism to meet a motion requirement - Graphical synthesis — inversion, poles, three/four positions - Analytical synthesis — dyads, Chebyshev spacing, Freudenstein
  3. Gears — Gear Formula Bank — involute geometry, interference, contact ratio, gear trains, epicyclic trains
  4. Cams, flywheels and balancing — motion from a prescribed law, and smoothing or cancelling the forces it causes - Cam Motion Laws and the Flat-Face Follower — cycloidal law, profile, curvature, driving-effort eccentricity - Flywheel Sizing from the Turning-Moment Diagram — mean torque, ΔE, Cs​, mass - Balancing of Rotors - Static and Dynamic — force and couple polygons, two-plane replacement
Mechanism given ──► Velocity / acceleration analysis ──► (loads, power)
Motion required ──► Synthesis ──► mechanism ──► analysis to check
Speed ratio required ──► Gears ──► train value ──► torques

Exam patterns

  • Cam, "draw profile / find v, a, ρ" — recognise by a lift table plus a named law. Needs s,s′,s′′ at one angle; flat face: ρ=R0​+s+s′′, ε=s′−e. Offset never changes a flat-face outline.
  • Flywheel from a torque diagram — recognise by triangle areas and a scale. Needs Tmean​=W/(cycle angle), then the area above the mean line, then I=ΔE/(ω2Cs​).
  • Balance "in other planes" — recognise by trial masses or inaccessible planes. Needs a couple polygon about one plane, a force polygon for the other, and care with add versus remove.
