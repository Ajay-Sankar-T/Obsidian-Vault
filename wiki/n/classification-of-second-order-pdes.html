Classification of Second Order PDEs

differential-equationspdesconceptclassificationhyperbolic

Related: PDEs - MOC · Canonical Forms of Second Order PDEs · D'Alembert's Solution of the Wave Equation · Separation of Variables · solved in Assignment 8 - First Order PDEs and Canonical Forms - Solutions Q4, Q6(e)

What it is

A second-order linear PDE in two variables,

auxx​+buxy​+cuyy​+dux​+euy​+fu=g(a,b,c,… functions of x,y),

has a type set by the discriminant of its principal part:

b2−4ac Type Model Behaviour
>0 hyperbolic utt​=c2uxx​ waves, finite speed, two real characteristic families
=0 parabolic ut​=kuxx​ diffusion, one characteristic family
<0 elliptic uxx​+uyy​=0 steady state, smoothing, no real characteristics

The classes need different boundary data: hyperbolic and parabolic equations are initial-value (evolution) problems, elliptic equations are boundary-value problems.

Why the discriminant

The type is the character of the quadratic form aξ2+bξη+cη2 (indefinite / semi-definite / definite). Under any change of variables ξ=ξ(x,y), η=η(x,y) with Jacobian J=0,

B2−4AC=J2(b2−4ac),

so the sign cannot change: the type is a property of the equation, not of the coordinates. (Proof: the coefficient matrix transforms as M′=JMJT with J=∂(ξ,η)/∂(x,y), hence detM′=J2detM and detM=ac−b2/4.)

Variable coefficients: the type can differ from region to region. The Tricomi equation yuxx​+uyy​=0 has b2−4ac=−4y: elliptic for y>0, parabolic on y=0, hyperbolic for y<0 (the equation of transonic flow).

Only the highest-order terms decide the type: yuxx​+2uxy​+uyy​=uux​+uy2​ has type given by 4−4y even though the right side is nonlinear.

Characteristics

Solving ady2−bdxdy+cdx2=0 gives the characteristic curves

dxdy​=2ab±b2−4ac​​.

Real and distinct (hyperbolic), real and equal (parabolic), complex conjugate (elliptic). Across a characteristic the second derivatives of a solution are not determined by the data: discontinuities (wave fronts) propagate along characteristics.

Method

  1. Read a,b,c from the second-order terms only.
  2. Compute Δ=b2−4ac as a function of (x,y).
  3. Solve Δ>0, Δ=0, Δ<0 to get the regions; sketch them.

Traps
  • Using b for the coefficient of uxy​, not of ux​.
  • Treating uxy​ and uyx​ as separate terms: the coefficient of uxy​ already includes both.
  • Quoting one type for the whole plane when the coefficients vary.

Cross-domain links

  • Eigenvalues, Rotation and Diagonalization Everywhere — the type is the signature of the symmetric matrix (ab/2​b/2c​): two eigenvalues of opposite sign (hyperbolic), one zero (parabolic), same sign (elliptic). It is the same classification as for conics (hyperbola, parabola, ellipse).
  • Mohr's Circle for Stress — the same symmetric 2×2 matrix machinery: stress principal axes are its eigenvectors, and B2−4AC=J2(b2−4ac) says the determinant of such a matrix is multiplied by the positive factor J2 under a change of basis, so its sign (the type) survives.
