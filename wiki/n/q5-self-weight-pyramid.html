AM2200 Tutorial 5 — Q5: Self-Weight Deformation of a Pyramid

Q5 asks for the displacement of the lower tip of a square pyramid due to its own weight when suspended vertically. Height L, top square b0​×b0​, specific weight γ, Young's modulus E.

1. Theory

For members under self-weight:

δ=∫0L​EA(x)N(x)​dx​

where N(x)=weight of material below the section.

2. Geometry and Area

Taking x measured downward from the top:

  • Side length: b(x)=b0​LL−x​
  • Area: A(x)=L2b02​(L−x)2​

3. Axial Force N(x)

The portion below x is a smaller pyramid with height (L−x). Volume: V(x)=3L2b02​(L−x)3​ Weight: N(x)=3L2γb02​(L−x)3​​

4. Solution

δ=∫0L​EL2b02​(L−x)2​3L2γb02​(L−x)3​​dx=3Eγ​∫0L​(L−x)dx

δ=3Eγ​[Lx−2x2​]0L​=3Eγ​2L2​=6EγL2​​

Physical Insight: The base size b0​ cancels out because both weight and area scale with b02​.
