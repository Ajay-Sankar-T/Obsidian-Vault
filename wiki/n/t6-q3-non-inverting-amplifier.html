T6-Q3 — Non-inverting Amplifier

EE1100op-amptutorial-solutionnon-inverting-amplifier

Hub: Tutorial 6 on OP-AMP · Previous: T6-Q2 - Inverting Amplifier · Next: T6-Q4 - Inverting Summer

Fig. 3: the input voltage is −0.5 V; the feedback resistor is 15 kΩ and the resistor from the inverting input to ground is 6.8 kΩ. Find (a) the voltage gain, (b) the output voltage. [3.2058, −1.6029 V]

Tests: non-inverting amplifier, Voltage Divider Rule.

Given

Input to the + terminal; Rf​=15 kΩ (output to −); Rg​=6.8 kΩ (− to ground); Vin​=−0.5 V.

Principle and why it applies

The input drives the non-inverting pin, and the feedback divides the output down to the inverting pin. Rule: v−​=v+​=Vin​, and the divider gives v−​=Vo​Rg​+Rf​Rg​​.

Solution

Vo​=Vin​(1+Rg​Rf​​).

(a) Av​=1+6.815​=1+2.2059=3.2059​. (b) Vo​=3.2059×(−0.5)=−1.603 V​.

Result

Av​=3.206 (positive: same polarity), Vo​=−1.603 V.

Check

Divider: Vo​21.86.8​=−1.603×0.3119=−0.5 V =Vin​ ✓. The gain is always ≥1 for this configuration.

Trap / alternative

Using the inverting formula −Rf​/Rg​=−2.2: the input is on the + terminal here, so the sign is positive and the "1 +" appears. The sheet's 3.2058 is rounding of 3.20588.
