AM2200 Tutorial 5 — Q8: Elastically Supported Bar

A uniform axial bar of length a, rigidity EA, is supported by two axial springs c1​ and c2​. A constant distributed load n acts along the bar. c1​=2c2​=2aEA​.

1. Theory

  • Differential equilibrium: dxdN​+n=0⇒N(x)=C−nx.
  • Constitutive relation: N=EAdxdu​.
  • Boundary conditions:
  • Left: N(0)=c1​u(0)
  • Right: N(a)=−c2​u(a)

2. Solution

Displacement u(x)

Integrating u′(x)=EAC−nx​:

u(x)=u(0)+EACx​−2EAnx2​

Using u(0)=C/c1​:

u(x)=c1​C​+EACx​−2EAnx2​

Solving for C

Using the right boundary condition N(a)=−c2​u(a):

C−na=−c2​(c1​C​+EACa​−2EAna2​)

Substituting c1​=2aEA​ and c2​=4aEA​ simplifies to:

C=149na​

Normal Force Distribution

N(x)=n(149a​−x)​

  • N(0)=149na​ (Tension)
  • N(a)=−145na​ (Compression)
  • Zero-force point: x=149a​
