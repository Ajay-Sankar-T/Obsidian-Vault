EE1100 Tutorial 4 — Q11: Star Connection Impedance

A 208V three-phase generator supplies total P=1800W at a line current IL​=10A. Identical impedances in star connection.

1. Theory

  • Star Connection: IL​=Iph​. Vph​=VL​/3​.
  • Total Power: Ptotal​=3Vph​Iph​cosϕ=3​VL​IL​cosϕ.

2. Solution

VL​=208V,IL​=10A,Ptotal​=1800W. Ptotal​=3​×208×10×cosϕ 1800=3602.6×cosϕ⇒cosϕ=3602.61800​≈0.4996≈0.5. ϕ=cos−1(0.5)=60∘.

Phase Impedance Z: Vph​=3​208​≈120.1V. Iph​=IL​=10A. ∣Z∣=Iph​Vph​​=10120.1​=12.01Ω.

Components: R=∣Z∣cosϕ=12.01×0.5≈6Ω​. X=∣Z∣sinϕ=12.01×sin(60∘)=12.01×0.866≈10.39Ω​.
