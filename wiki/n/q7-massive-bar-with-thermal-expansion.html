AM2200 Tutorial 5 — Q7: Massive Bar with Thermal Expansion

A uniform bar of weight W0​, area A, height h, and expansion coefficient αT​ is fixed at the top and touches the ground. We analyze the effect of temperature increase ΔT.

1. Theory

Total strain: ϵ=Eσ​+αT​ΔT​. Compatibility: Since both ends are fixed (top support and ground), total elongation is zero:

∫0h​ϵdx=0​

2. Solution

Axial Force N(x)

Let R be the ground reaction. At distance x from top:

N(x)=hW0​​(h−x)−R

σ(x)=AhW0​​(h−x)−AR​

Applying Compatibility

∫0h​(Eσ(x)​+αT​ΔT)dx=0

Evaluating the integral leads to: R=2W0​​+AEαT​ΔT.

Stress Distribution

Substituting R:

σ(x)=AW0​​(21​−hx​)−EαT​ΔT​

Compression Everywhere

To have σ(x)≤0 everywhere, the max stress at x=0 must be ≤0:

2AW0​​−EαT​ΔT≤0⇒ΔTmin​=2AEαT​W0​​​
