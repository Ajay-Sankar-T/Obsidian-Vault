T4-Q12: Star Load: Phase Currents and Line Voltage

EE1100tutorial-solutionthree-phasestar-connectionphasors

Hub: Tutorial 4 - Resonance, Filter and Three-Phase Circuits · Theory: Theory - Three-Phase Circuits · Previous: T4-Q11 - Star Load Impedance from Power · Next tutorial: Tutorial 5 - Diode Circuits and Applications

A balanced three-phase star-connected load has an impedance of 4∠60∘ Ω from line to neutral, and Vˉan​=20∠30∘ V. (a) The currents in phases a, b, c. (b) The voltage from line b to neutral. (c) The phasor Vˉac​. [Sheet: 5∠−30∘, 5∠−150∘, 5∠90∘ A; 20∠−90∘ V; 34.64∠0∘ V]

Tests: star connection, ±120∘ symmetry, line voltage =3​× phase voltage at a 30∘ shift.

Given

Z=4∠60∘ Ω in each phase (the printed tutorial text reads "4∣60∘"), Vˉan​=20∠30∘ V, abc sequence.

Principle and why it applies

Each phase of a star load is a plain single-phase circuit: Iˉa​=Vˉan​/Z. The balanced source gives Vˉbn​ and Vˉcn​ by shifting −120∘ and +120∘, and the line voltage is a difference of phase voltages: Vˉac​=Vˉan​−Vˉcn​.

Diagram

Solution

(a)

Iˉa​=4∠60∘20∠30∘​=5∠−30∘ A,Iˉb​=5∠(−30∘−120∘)=5∠−150∘ A,Iˉc​=5∠(−30∘+120∘)=5∠90∘ A.

(b) Vˉbn​=20∠(30∘−120∘)=20∠−90∘ V (and Vˉcn​=20∠150∘ V).

(c) Vˉac​=Vˉan​−Vˉcn​=(17.32+j10)−(−17.32+j10)=34.64+j0=34.64∠0∘ V. In magnitude VL​=3​Vp​=3​×20=34.64 V.

Result

Iˉa​=5∠−30∘, Iˉb​=5∠−150∘, Iˉc​=5∠90∘ A;Vˉbn​=20∠−90∘ V;Vˉac​=34.64∠0∘ V​

Check

The three phase currents sum to zero: 5∠−30∘+5∠−150∘+5∠90∘=(4.33−j2.5)+(−4.33−j2.5)+(0+j5)=0 ✓ (no neutral current). Power: P=3I2R=3×25×4cos60∘=150 W =3​×34.64×5×0.5 ✓.

Trap / alternative

  • An earlier version of this note read the impedance as 46∠0∘ Ω (a misreading of "4∣60∘") and so could not reproduce the printed answers; with Z=4∠60∘ Ω every printed answer is reproduced exactly.
  • Vˉac​ points the opposite way to Vˉca​: Vˉca​=Vˉcn​−Vˉan​=34.64∠180∘, so Vˉac​=34.64∠0∘.
  • In a star, the line voltage leads the phase voltage by 30∘ only for the right pair (Vˉab​ leads Vˉan​); Vˉac​ lags Vˉan​ by 30∘ here (0∘ against 30∘).
