T2-Q10: Series RLC Step Response and Damping Ranges

EE1100tutorial-solutionDC-circuitstransientsRLC

Hub: Tutorial 2 - Transient Analysis of DC Circuits · Theory: Theory - Transients in RL, RC and RLC Circuits · Previous: T2-Q9 - Switch Closes in an RC Network · Next: T2-Q11 - Second-Order Equation with Initial Conditions

A series RLC circuit with a 100 V DC source, R=50 Ω, L=0.1 H, C=50 μF. Determine the critical resistance Rc​, damping factor ζ, natural frequency ωn​, damped frequency ωd​. If the switch closes at t=0 with zero initial conditions, find i, vR​, vL​, vC​. Classify the response and give the range of R for overdamped, critically damped, underdamped and undamped behaviour. [Sheet: Rc​=89.44 Ω, ζ=0.559, ωn​=447.2, ωd​=370.8 rad/s; i=2.7e−250tsin371t A; underdamped]

Tests: series RLC step response, damping classification.

Given

V=100 V, R=50 Ω, L=0.1 H, C=50 μF, i(0)=0, vC​(0)=0.

Principle and why it applies

KVL after the switch closes: Ldtdi​+Ri+vC​=V with i=CdvC​/dt. The initial conditions are zero, so dtdi​(0+)=LV​ (the whole 100 V appears across L at 0+). The final state is i∞​=0, vC∞​=V (the capacitor blocks DC). The damping ratio ζ=R/Rc​ tells us the shape.

Solution

ωn​=LC​1​=0.1×50×10−6​1​=447.2 rad/s,α=2LR​=250 s−1,

Rc​=2CL​​=22000​=89.44 Ω,ζ=Rc​R​=ωn​α​=0.559,ωd​=ωn2​−α2​=370.8 rad/s.

ζ<1: underdamped (also R=50<Rc​).

Current: i=ωd​LV​e−αtsinωd​t=370.8×0.1100​e−250tsin371t:

i=2.70e−250tsin371t A.

Element voltages:

vR​=Ri=135e−250tsin371t V,

vL​=Ldtdi​=e−250t(100cos371t−67.4sin371t) V(=0.1e−250t[1001.7cos371t−675sin371t] V),

vC​=V−vR​−vL​=100−e−250t(100cos371t+67.4sin371t) V.

Ranges of R (with L=0.1 H, C=50 μF fixed):

Response Condition
overdamped R>Rc​=89.44 Ω
critically damped R=89.44 Ω
underdamped 0<R<89.44 Ω
undamped (pure oscillation at 447.2 rad/s) R=0

Result

Rc​=89.44 Ω, ζ=0.559, ωn​=447.2, ωd​=370.8 rad/s;  i=2.70e−250tsin371t A; underdamped​

Figure: (top) the element voltages sum to 100 V at every instant; (bottom) vC​ for four resistances: the capacitor overshoots 100 V when underdamped (the ringing), approaches it without overshoot at critical damping, and slowly when over-damped.

Check

Overshoot of vC​: e−πα/ωd​=e−2.12=12 %, peak ≈112 V. At t=0+: vL​=100 V, vR​=0, vC​=0 ✓ (KVL). Final: i→0, vC​→100 V ✓.

Trap / alternative

  • Writing the amplitude of the current as V/L=1000 (the critically damped coefficient) instead of V/(ωd​L)=2.70 A.
  • The sheet's vC​ is printed in a factored form with a 0.269 coefficient; it is the same function (0.269×371=100, 0.269×250=67.3).
  • "Undamped" requires R=0, not R→∞; large R is overdamped (sluggish, not oscillatory).
