T2-Q5: RL Circuit with a Switch That Opens at t = 0

EE1100tutorial-solutionDC-circuitstransientsRL-circuit

Hub: Tutorial 2 - Transient Analysis of DC Circuits · Theory: Theory - Transients in RL, RC and RLC Circuits · Previous: T2-Q4 - Source-Free RL Decay · Next: T2-Q6 - RC Circuit with a Two-Position Switch

Find v(t) for t>0 (the voltage across the 0.5 H inductor). Hint: the current source may be converted to an equivalent voltage source. The circuit has a 2 A source with 12 Ω, a switch at t=0, 20 Ω, 6 Ω, 5 Ω, a 20 V source and L=0.5 H. [Sheet: v=vL​=−4e−20t V]

Tests: the five-step switching recipe, Thevenin equivalent seen by L, source transformation.

Given

2 A source in parallel with 12 Ω; the switch connects this pair to node S; at S: 20 Ω to ground, 5 Ω in series with a 20 V source (+ at top) to ground, and the branch 6 Ω + L (voltage v, + at top). The switch has been closed for a long time and opens at t=0 (this reading reproduces the sheet's answer; a switch that closed at t=0 would give a different time constant).

Principle and why it applies

iL​ cannot jump, so the story is: find iL​(0−) in the closed-switch DC steady state; find the final value and τ in the new (open-switch) circuit; then iL​(t)=i∞​+[iL​(0+)−i∞​]e−t/τ and v=LdiL​/dt. After the switch opens, the 2 A source and its 12 Ω are disconnected and drop out entirely.

Diagram

Solution

Before (t<0, switch closed, L a short): all of S's neighbours act at once. KCL at S (VS​ unknown, the 6 Ω ends on the shorted inductor):

2=12VS​​+20VS​​+5VS​−20​+6VS​​ ⇒ 0.5VS​=6 ⇒ VS​=12 V,iL​(0−)=612​=2 A.

(The hint gives the same VS​: the source transformation of 2 A ∥ 12 Ω is 24 V in series with 12 Ω.)

After (t>0, switch open): the inductor sees 6 Ω in series with the Thevenin equivalent of 20 Ω and (5 Ω + 20 V):

Rth​=20∥5=4 Ω,Vth​=20⋅20+520​=16 V.

Rtot​=6+4=10 Ω,τ=Rtot​L​=100.5​=0.05 s,i∞​=1016​=1.6 A.

iL​(t)=1.6+(2−1.6)e−20t=1.6+0.4e−20t A.

v(t)=LdtdiL​​=0.5×(−20)(0.4)e−20t=−4e−20t V.

Result

v(t)=vL​(t)=−4e−20t V,t>0​

Check

At t=0+ the inductor current is still 2 A but the new circuit would settle at 1.6 A, so the inductor must push back with a negative voltage: v(0+)=−4 V. KVL at 0+: the Thevenin source (16 V) minus the drop across 10 Ω (2×10=20 V) leaves −4 V across L ✓.

Trap / alternative

  • The inductor voltage is not continuous: it was 0 V (a short) before and jumps to −4 V.
  • Don't include the 2 A source and 12 Ω in the post-switch circuit; the open switch isolates them.
  • Equally valid: write Ldi/dt+10i=16 and solve the first-order ODE directly.

Concepts used

  • Continuity of iL​, Thevenin equivalent seen by the storage element, first-order response (Theory - Transients in RL, RC and RLC Circuits, Theory - Circuit Laws and Network Theorems)
