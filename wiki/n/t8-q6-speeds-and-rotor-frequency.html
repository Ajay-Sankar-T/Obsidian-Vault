T8-Q6: Synchronous Speed, Rotor Speed and Rotor Frequency

EE1100tutorial-solutionmachinesinduction-machineslip

Hub: Tutorial 8 - Induction Machines · Theory: Theory - Three-Phase Induction Machines · Previous: T8-Q5 - Torque-Slip Characteristic · Next: T8-Q7 - Relative Speeds of the Fields

A 3-phase induction motor has 4 poles and is supplied by a 50 Hz balanced 3-phase voltage; it runs at a slip of 6 %. Determine (a) the synchronous speed in rpm, (b) the rotor speed in rpm, (c) the frequency of voltage and current in the rotor circuit. [Sheet: 1500 rpm, 1410 rpm, 3 Hz]

Tests: ns​=120f/P, slip, f2​=sf1​.

Given

P=4, f1​=50 Hz, s=0.06.

Principle and why it applies

The stator field turns at ns​=120f/P irrespective of load; the rotor lags by the slip, nr​=(1−s)ns​; the rotor conductors see the field sweep past at the difference speed sns​, so the rotor emf and current have frequency f2​=sf1​.

Solution

(a) ns​=4120×50​=1500 rpm.

(b) nr​=(1−s)ns​=(1−0.06)×1500=1410 rpm.

(c) f2​=sf1​=0.06×50=3 Hz.

Result

ns​=1500 rpm,nr​=1410 rpm,f2​=3 Hz​

Check

Relative speed ns​−nr​=90 rpm =1.5 rev/s; with 2 pole pairs the rotor sees 2×1.5=3 cycles per second ✓ =f2​.

Trap / alternative

The rotor frequency is not 50 Hz (that is the standstill value, s=1) but 3 Hz. Always use the number of poles, P=4, in ns​; and write the slip as a fraction (0.06) in the products.

Concepts used

  • Synchronous speed, slip, rotor frequency (Induction Motor - Slip, Rotor Circuit and Power Flow, Revolving Magnetic Field and Synchronous Speed)
