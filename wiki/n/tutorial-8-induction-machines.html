Exam-pattern hints

Theory Q1–Q5 are answered with a labelled sketch (the revolving-field proof and the torque–slip curve with its regions). Numerical questions are marked on (i) ns​ from the poles, (ii) using s in the rotor quantities, (iii) torque from Pg​/ωs​, (iv) the a2 factor when referring the rotor resistance, and (v) the Y/Δ phase-current bookkeeping of Q9.

Sheet answers that differ from a direct calculation

Everything reproduces to the digits printed, within rounding, with these notes:

  • Q9: nr​=1467.9 rpm (sheet 1468.2), core loss 163.2 W (sheet 163.3), η=89.3 % (sheet 89.2 %): all rounding.
  • Q10: efficiency depends on the loss bookkeeping: 90.2 % from the circuit's input power, 89.7 % with the stator copper on the full line current (sheet 89.6 %); output 48.15 hp (sheet 48.25 hp).
  • Q11: 71.0 A, 274.3 N·m, 469.7 N·m, 0.5455 Ω against the sheet's 71.06, 274.12, 469.1, 0.5452 (≤ 0.2 %); output at Tmax​ 63.6 kW against 63.5 kW.
