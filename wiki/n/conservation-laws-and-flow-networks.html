Conservation Laws and Flow Networks

synthesiscross-domainconservation-lawskclcontinuity

← up: Cross-Domain Synthesis MOC

Every network in the vault has the same two-law skeleton: a node law (what flows in flows out) and a loop law (potential differences around a closed path sum to zero), glued together by an element law. This note lines them up for circuits, pipes and structures.

The three networks

Circuit Pipe flow Structure (bars and rigid links)
through quantity (conserved at a node) current I volume/mass flow Q, m˙ force F
across quantity (sums to zero in a loop) voltage V pressure/head p displacement δ
node law KCL continuity ∑m˙in​=∑m˙out​ equilibrium ∑F=0
loop law KVL Bernoulli between two points compatibility (deformations fit)
dissipative element R friction head loss hf​ —
stores "potential" capacitor 21​CV2 tank, pressure/elevation p+ρgz spring/bar 21​kδ2
stores "kinetic" inductor 21​LI2 moving fluid 21​ρV2 mass 21​mv2

Sources: Basic Laws, Conservation of Mass, Bernoulli Formula Bank, Statically Indeterminate Axial Members.

A warning about the dictionary. The Mechanical-Electrical Analogy uses force ↔ voltage, which is right for one oscillator equation. For networks of joints and members, equilibrium is a node law, so force must be the through variable (force ↔ current, displacement ↔ voltage, stiffness ↔ conductance). Pick the analogy by the job, and say which one you are using.

What the table buys you

  • Force sharing is a current divider. Bars in parallel share one displacement and their forces add, so each carries a share proportional to its stiffness AE/L, exactly as parallel conductances split a current (Voltage Divider Rule and its dual). Springs in parallel add stiffness like conductances.
  • Statically indeterminate = a circuit KCL alone cannot solve. Equilibrium gives the node law, compatibility the loop law; you need both, as in Tutorial 5 (bolt and tube).
  • Bernoulli is KVL for an ideal pipe. Between two points, 21​ρV2+p+ρgz is a conserved sum like a loop of ideal elements; adding hf​ turns the loop law into KVL with resistors. 21​ρV2 is the inductor energy: for a pipe segment of length ℓ and area A, inertance Lh​=ρℓ/A and Q=AV give 21​Lh​Q2=21​ρV2(Aℓ), energy density 21​ρV2.
  • A tank is a capacitor. For an open tank of area A with p=ρgh, Q=Adh/dt=(A/ρg)dp/dt, so Ch​=A/ρg. Stored mass (like charge) cannot jump; only the rate of change is set by the net flow.

Correction to carry forward

The continuity note's cross-domain paragraph pairs a filling tank with inductor current continuity. The stored conserved quantity there is mass, whose electrical twin is charge on a capacitor, so the matching rule is vC​(0+)=vC​(0−) (AC Transients). Inertia of moving fluid is what matches the inductor. That note has been adjusted accordingly.

Where energy enters

KVL, Bernoulli and compatibility all hide an energy balance, which is why Energy Methods — A Unified View and the second-order system reappear whenever a network has both a "potential" store and a "kinetic" store.
