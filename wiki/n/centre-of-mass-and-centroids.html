Choosing dm

  • Thin wire: dm=λdx (line density λ)
  • Thin plate: dm=σdA (areal density σ)
  • 3D solid: dm=ρdV

For a uniform body, dm∝dV (or dA), so xCM​=∫dV∫xdV​​ and centre of mass=centroid​ — the two only coincide when density is uniform; for a non-uniform body (e.g. a ship's hull with concentrated machinery), the centre of gravity G used in Stability of Floating Bodies is generally not the geometric centroid of the hull's volume.

Symmetry shortcut

If an object has a symmetry axis/plane, the COM lies on it:

  • Uniform rod: xCM​=L/2
  • Rectangle: (xCM​,yCM​)=(b/2,h/2)
  • Circle/sphere/cylinder: geometric centre (along the axis, for a cylinder)
  • Triangle: intersection of medians, at h/3 from the base (or 2h/3 from the vertex)
