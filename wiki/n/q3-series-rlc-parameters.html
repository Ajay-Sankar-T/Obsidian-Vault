EE1100 Tutorial 4 — Q3: Series RLC Parameters

A voltage e(t)=10sin(ωt) is applied to a series RLC circuit. At resonance: VC,max​=500V, BW=400rad/s, Zres​=100Ω.

1. Theory

  • At resonance, Z=R. Thus R=100Ω.
  • VC​=Q×Vsource​ at resonance.
  • BW=LR​.
  • ω0​=LC​1​.

2. Solution

(a) Resonant Frequency ω0​ Vsource,max​=10V. Q=Vsource,max​VC,max​​=10500​=50. Since BW=Qω0​​: ω0​=BW×Q=400×50=20,000rad/s​.

(b) Upper and Lower Limits of Bandwidth ω1,2​=ω0​∓2BW​=20,000∓200.

  • Lower limit: 19,800rad/s​
  • Upper limit: 20,200rad/s​

(c) Values of L and C From BW=LR​: L=BWR​=400100​=0.25H​. From ω02​=LC1​: C=ω02​L1​=(20,000)2×0.251​=4×108×0.251​=1081​=0.01μF​.
