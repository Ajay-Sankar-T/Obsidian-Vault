RLC Circuits — Responses (Series/Parallel, DC/AC/Step)

EE1100RLCgraphs

← up: MOC

This folder collects every flavor of RLC response the course touches — series vs parallel, DC vs AC, step vs natural vs steady-state — into one place, with plotted graphs (not just formulas) so the shapes of the responses can be compared directly, not just their equations. It draws together material otherwise spread across Tutorial 2, AC Transients, and Week 8.

The four axes of variation

Every RLC response in this course sits somewhere on two independent axes:

                    SERIES  ◄──────────────►  PARALLEL
                 (R,L,C share current)    (R,L,C share voltage)

    DC/step  ┌─────────────────────┬─────────────────────┐
    (natural │  Series RLC step    │  Parallel RLC        │
    response)│  response           │  natural response     │
             ├─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┤
    AC       │  Series RLC AC      │  Parallel RLC AC      │
    (forced +│  transient + steady │  transient + steady   │
    natural) │  state              │  state                 │
             └─────────────────────┴─────────────────────┘

Once you know which cell of this grid a problem is in, the solution method is fixed — see Series vs Parallel RLC — Key Distinctions for how to tell them apart, and DC Step Response / AC Transient and Steady-State Response for the two "rows."
