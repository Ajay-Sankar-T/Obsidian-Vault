Pattern recognition

You see Do
"after a long time", "steady state" C→ open, L→ short; resistive circuit (Q2, Q3)
a switch operates, one L or C five-step recipe: x(0−), continuity, x∞​, τ=Rth​C or L/Rth​ (Q5, Q6, Q8, Q9)
"time for 50 % of the energy" energy ∝e−2t/τ, so t=2τ​ln2 (Q4)
vo​ or iC​ at 0+ they jump; recompute from the new circuit with vC​(0+) as a source (Q8, Q9)
L and C both present compare R with Rc​=2L/C​ (series) (Q7, Q10)
an integro-differential equation read off L, R, 1/C, and the integral's initial value as vC​(0) (Q11)
a current source into R∥L∥C parallel: α=1/2RC (Q12)

Exam-pattern hints

Switching problems give marks for (i) the right initial condition (only vC​, iL​ carry over), (ii) the right τ from the new circuit, and (iii) sign: say whether a quantity is discharging. Second-order answers are checked for the amplitude factor 1/(ωd​L) or 1/(ωd​C) and for classifying damping by R versus Rc​. Q1 is a graph-reading question: label every corner value.

Sheet answers that differ from a direct calculation

  • Q8: printed vo​(1.3 ms)=25.6 V; it must decay with τ=2.5 ms: 15.2 V.
  • Q9: printed vC​(1.3 ms)=70.0 V; τ=0.833 ms gives 78.99 V.
  • Q7: the sheet's figure (10 µF, 100 Ω, 1 H) and its text (5 µF, 200 Ω, 2 H) differ; the printed answer matches the text and is the one solved.
  • Q12: printed ωd​=33119.1 and 37.74 V use ζ rounded to 0.35; exact 33072 and 37.80 V. (The sheet also says "three-phase", which is a slip: the circuit is a single parallel RLC.)
  • All other printed answers match.
