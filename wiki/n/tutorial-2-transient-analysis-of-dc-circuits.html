Tutorial 2 — Transient Analysis of DC Circuits

EE1100tutorialDC-circuitstransients

← up: MOC · builds on: Tutorial 1

Covers first-order (single L or C) and second-order (RLC) transient response — switching circuits into a new state and finding how voltage/current evolve afterward.

Core concepts used

  • Capacitors and Inductors — v-i relations, energy storage, continuity of v_C and i_L
  • RC/RL Transients and Time Constant — the x(t) = x_∞ + (x_0−x_∞)e^(−t/τ) template, and second-order damping (over/critical/underdamped)
  • Thevenin's theorem — used to reduce the resistive network around the storage element down to a single R_th before computing τ
