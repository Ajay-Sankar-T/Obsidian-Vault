T7-Q8 — Voltage Regulation at Full Load

EE1100transformertutorial-solutionvoltage-regulation

Hub: Tutorial 7 on Transformer · Previous: T7-Q7 - OC and SC Test Parameters · Next: T7-Q9 - Efficiency and Regulation from Tests

A 20 kVA, 2500/500 V, 50 Hz transformer: HV r1​=8 Ω, x1​=17 Ω; LV r2​=0.3 Ω, x2​=0.7 Ω. Find the voltage regulation and the secondary terminal voltage at full load for a power factor of (a) 0.8 lagging, (b) 0.8 leading, with the primary at 2500 V. [(a) 10.59 %, 447.04 V (b) −2.65 %, 513.28 V]

Tests: regulation formula, leading versus lagging load.

Given

a=5, E2​=2500/5=500 V, I2​=50020000​=40 A.

Principle and why it applies

Refer to the LV side: Re2​=r2​+r1​/a2=0.3+258​=0.62 Ω, Xe2​=0.7+2517​=1.38 Ω. The regulation formula E2​−V2​≈I2​(Re2​cosϕ±Xe2​sinϕ) uses + for lagging, − for leading.

Solution

Drops: I2​Re2​=24.8 V, I2​Xe2​=55.2 V.

(a) 0.8 lag. E2​−V2​≈24.8(0.8)+55.2(0.6)=19.84+33.12=52.96 V.

VR=50052.96​=10.59 %,V2​=500−52.96=447.04 V.

(b) 0.8 lead. E2​−V2​≈19.84−33.12=−13.28 V.

VR=−2.66 %,V2​=500+13.28=513.28 V.

approximate (sheet) exact phasor sum
lag V2​=447.04 V, VR=10.59 % V2​=446.2 V (VR=10.76 %)
lead V2​=513.28 V, VR=−2.66 % V2​=509.8 V (VR=−1.96 %)

Result

(a) VR=10.59 %, V2​=447.0 V. (b) VR=−2.66 % (voltage rises), V2​=513.3 V.

Check

Exact: V1′2​=(V2​+Δr​)2+Δq2​ with Δr​=I(Rcosϕ±Xsinϕ), Δq​=I(Xcosϕ∓Rsinϕ); for the leading case the quadrature drop is large (59 V), so the approximation overestimates V2​ by 3.5 V (0.7 %). A leading (capacitive) load can raise the secondary voltage above the no-load value: the zero-regulation angle is −tan−1(Re2​/Xe2​)=−24.2∘.

Trap / alternative

Quoting the sign of VR for the leading case as positive. The quoted −2.65 % in the sheet is −13.28/500=−2.656 %. Exact regulation should be used when the quadrature drop is not small compared with V2​.
