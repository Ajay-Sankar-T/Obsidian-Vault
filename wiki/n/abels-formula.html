Abel's Formula

Related: Linear Independence and the Wronskian

From the Wronskian test proof: for solutions y1​,y2​ of y′′+P(x)y′+Q(x)y=0,

W′+PW=0(Abel’s Formula)

a first-order linear DE (see Linear Differential Equations) whose solution is

W(x)=Ce−∫P(x)dx.

Consequences

  • Since e−∫P(x)dx=0 always, W(x) and W(x0​) have the same sign (or both zero) — it suffices to check W at a single point x0​∈I.
  • If W(x0​)=0, then y1​,y2​ are independent throughout I.
  • If W(x0​)=0, then y1​,y2​ are dependent throughout I.
  • Abel's formula gives an explicit formula for W without solving the ODE fully.
  • Unlike arbitrary functions, for solutions of a linear ODE: W≡0⟺y1​,y2​ are linearly dependent — a much stronger dichotomy than for general functions.

Integrating W′+PW=0 from x0​ to x:

W(x)=W(x0​)e∫x0​x​P(t)dt.

Worked Examples

Verify independence and find GS: y1​=e2x,y2​=e−3x solve y′′+y′−6y=0 over R.

  • y1​: (4+2−6)e2x=0 ✓; y2​: (9−3−6)e−3x=0 ✓
  • W(y1​,y2​)=−3e−x−2e−x=−5e−x=0 for all x ⇒ independent.
  • GS: y=c1​e2x+c2​e−3x.

Repeated-root case: Show c1​e2x+c2​xe2x is the GS of y′′−4y′+4y=0.

  • y1​=e2x satisfies (4−8+4)e2x=0. y2​=xe2x: y2′​=e2x+2xe2x, y2′′​=4e2x+4xe2x, satisfies the DE.
  • W(y1​,y2​)=e2x(e2x+2xe2x)−xe2x(2e2x)=e4x=0.
  • GS: y=c1​e2x+c2​xe2x.

Variable-coefficient case: Show c1​x+c2​xlnx is the GS of x2y′′−xy′+y=0, x>0.

  • y1​=x: y1′​=1,y1′′​=0⇒0−x+x=0 ✓. y2​=xlnx: y2′​=lnx+1, y2′′​=1/x; substituting gives x−x(lnx+1)+xlnx=0 ✓.
  • W(y1​,y2​)=x(lnx+1)−xlnx=x=0 for x>0.
  • GS: y=c1​x+c2​xlnx.

Using Abel's formula directly: If xy′′−(x+1)y′+y=0 has W(1)=e, find W(x).

  • Standard form: y′′−xx+1​y′+x1​y=0⇒P(x)=−xx+1​.
  • W(x)=W(1)e−∫1x​P(t)dt=e⋅e∫1x​tt+1​dt.
  • ∫1x​tt+1​dt=(x+lnx)−1, so W(x)=e⋅ex−1+lnx=xex.
