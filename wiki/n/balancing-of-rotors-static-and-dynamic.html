Balancing of Rotors — Static and Dynamic

conceptKDoM

In one breath

A rotating mass m at radius r pulls the shaft with mrω2 in a direction that spins with it. A rotor is balanced when the vector sum of all mr is zero (no net force) and the vector sum of all mrz about any point is zero (no net couple). Any pile of unbalance can be replaced by an equivalent pair, one in each of two chosen planes.

Part of KDoMs - MOC. Solved in Tutorial-10-solution.

1. Conditions

For masses mi​ at radius ri​, angle θi​, axial position zi​:

∑mi​ri​ejθi​=0(force: static balance),∑mi​ri​zi​ejθi​=0(couple: dynamic balance)

  • Static balance alone (centre of mass on the axis) can leave a couple: two equal masses on opposite sides at different z balance on knife edges but wobble when spun.
  • Both conditions are needed. Rotating unbalance force is ∑mrω2, a rotating couple is ω2∑mrz.

2. The two-plane replacement

Choose two planes P and Q (say z=0 and z=L). A mass system of unbalance Ui​=mi​ri​ejθi​ at zi​ is equivalent in force and couple to UP​ and UQ​ with

UP​+UQ​=∑Ui​,UQ​L=∑Ui​zi​⇒UQ​=L1​∑Ui​zi​,UP​=∑Ui​−UQ​

Then m=∣U∣/r at that plane's radius, at angle argU. This is how a correction is moved from an inaccessible plane to one you can reach.

Add or remove?

Adding mass m at angle θ is the same as removing mass m at θ+180∘. A rotor with trial masses attached that runs smooth has unbalance −(trial) without them. To balance it, fit the equivalent of the trial masses in the new planes, or take the same amount off the opposite side.

3. Method on paper

  1. Draw the end view with angles from a single reference; write each mr.
  2. Take moments about one plane; close the couple polygon (vectors mrz) to get the other plane's vector.
  3. Close the force polygon for the first plane.
  4. Convert vectors to mass and angle; state the angle convention and whether it is added or removed.

4. Traps and checks

Traps
  • Signs of z: planes on either side of the reference have opposite sign.
  • Mixing radii: a vector is mr, so a 10 cm plane needs ∣U∣/10, not ∣U∣/15.
  • Angle sense: say "clockwise from A1​" or "anticlockwise from the x axis" and stay with it.
  • Reporting the added position when the question asks for material removed (or the reverse).
Check: how do you know the answer is right?

Re-sum the final system: force and couple polygons must close. Also the two-plane method must give the same answer whichever plane you take moments about.

5. Connections

  • Rotating Vectors - Phasors, Velocity Polygons and Complex Kinematics — unbalance vectors add like phasors, and a closed polygon is a zero sum.
  • Vibration of Particles and Rigid Bodies — unbalance is the forcing term of a rotating-unbalance vibration; the response peaks at the critical speed.
  • Mass Moments of Inertia — a rotor's principal axis misaligned with the shaft is the same couple unbalance, seen through the inertia tensor's products.
