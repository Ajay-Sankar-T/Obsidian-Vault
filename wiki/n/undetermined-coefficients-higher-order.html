Undetermined Coefficients - Higher Order

Related: Higher Order Homogeneous ODEs · Undetermined Coefficients · Variation of Parameters - 3rd Order

Goal

Find yp​ for an​y(n)+⋯+a1​y′+a0​y=R(x) with constant coefficients. Same restriction as the 2nd order case: R(x) must be polynomial, exponential, sine/cosine, or products/sums of these.

yc​)">

Standard trial forms (no duplication with yc​)

  • R(x)=Pm​(x) ⇒ yp​=Am​xm+⋯+A0​ (0 not a root of AE)
  • R(x)=aekx ⇒ yp​=Aekx (k not a root of AE)
  • R(x)=acosβx or asinβx ⇒ yp​=Acosβx+Bsinβx (iβ not a root of AE)
  • R(x)=Pm​(x)ekx ⇒ yp​=(Am​xm+⋯+A0​)ekx
  • R(x)=ekx(acosβx+bsinβx) ⇒ yp​=ekx(Acosβx+Bsinβx) (k+iβ not a root)

Modification Rule (key difference from 2nd order)

Let s be the multiplicity of the matching root (k, or k±iβ) in the auxiliary equation. In higher order equations, s can be 2, 3, or more (not just 0 or 1). Rule: multiply the standard trial form by xs.

  • Example: a root of multiplicity 3 matching ekx forces yp​=Ax3ekx.
  • For product forcing Pm​(x)ekx with duplication: trial is xs(Am​xm+⋯+A0​)ekx.
  • Superposition still applies: split R=R1​+R2​, solve separately, add.

Useful trick

For forcing ekx matching a root k of multiplicity s, substitute y=ekxu(x). The equation reduces to u(s)⋅(const)=1 (roughly), so up​ is a degree-s monomial in x — avoids differentiating xsekx directly multiple times (see Problem 3 below).

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Solve the auxiliary equation; list roots and multiplicities.
  2. Write yc​.
  3. Choose trial form for yp​ matching R(x)'s type.
  4. Check for duplication with yc​; find multiplicity s, multiply by xs.
  5. Substitute, equate coefficients, solve.
  6. Write y=yc​+yp​; apply ICs if given.
