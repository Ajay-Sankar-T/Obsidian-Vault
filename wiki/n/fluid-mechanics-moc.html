The four biggest problem-solving ideas

Hydrostatics: P=ρgh​Curved surface: H→projection, V→imaginary-fluid weight​

Mass: Inflow−Outflow=Accumulation​Momentum: Force=Momentum out−Momentum in​

And for floating-body stability: IO​→BM=Vsub​IO​​→GM=BM−BG→GM>0⇒stable​.

Cross-domain synthesis

Continuity ↔ KCL, momentum/RTT ↔ Reynolds Transport bookkeeping shared with circuit nodal analysis, metacentric stability ↔ column buckling, centroids/second moments of area shared directly with beam bending — see Cross-Domain Synthesis for the fully worked connections across all vaults. Most relevant here: Conservation Laws and Flow Networks (continuity, Bernoulli ↔ KCL, KVL), Second Moments of Area (centre of pressure, metacentre), and Fluids and Solids (viscosity vs shear modulus, Deborah number).
