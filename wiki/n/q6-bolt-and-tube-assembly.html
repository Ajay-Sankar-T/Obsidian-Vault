AM2200 Tutorial 5 — Q6: Bolt and Tube Assembly

Q6 analyzes a 20 mm diameter bolt passing through a tube (ID 50 mm, OD 60 mm). A 40 kN force is applied. End caps are rigid.

1. Theory

This is a composite-member problem in parallel. Because end caps are rigid: δb​=δt​​. Since materials and lengths are identical: Ab​Fb​​=At​Ft​​​⇒∣σb​∣=∣σt​∣​.

2. Solution

Areas

  • Bolt: Ab​=4π​(20)2=314.16 mm2
  • Tube: At​=4π​(602−502)=863.94 mm2

Load Distribution

Fb​+Ft​=40 kN. Using σb​=−σt​=σ:

σ=Ab​+At​P​=314.16+863.9440000​=33.96 MPa​

  • Fb​=σAb​=33.96×314.16≈10.67 kN (Tension)
  • Ft​=σAt​=33.96×863.94≈29.33 kN (Compression)
