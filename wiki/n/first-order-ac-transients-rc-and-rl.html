First-Order AC Transients (RC and RL)

EE1100AC-circuitstransientsfirst-order

← up: AC Transients · builds on: RC/RL Transients and Time Constant, Impedance and Series-Parallel AC Circuits

Time-constant explorer

Universal first-order AC template

x(t)=xss​(t)+[x(0+)−xss​(0+)]e−t/τ​

The critical difference from the DC version in Week 4: there, xss​ (called x∞​) is a constant. Here, xss​(t) is a time-varying sinusoid, found from phasor analysis, so the "gap" x(0+)−xss​(0+) must be evaluated at t=0 using the sinusoid's own value there, not a DC final value.

RC circuit with an AC source

Series RC driven by vs​(t)=Vm​sinωt. Impedance Z=R−j/(ωC), so ∣Z∣=R2+1/(ωC)2​, θZ​=−tan−1(1/(ωCR)) (from Impedance and Series-Parallel AC Circuits). Steady-state current:

iss​(t)=Im​sin(ωt+ϕ),ϕ=−θZ​>0 (current leads)

Complete response:

i(t)=Im​sin(ωt+ϕ)+Ae−t/RC

Finding A: at t=0+, i(0+)=Im​sinϕ+A, so

A=i(0+)−Im​sinϕ

giving

i(t)=Im​sin(ωt+ϕ)+[i(0+)−Im​sinϕ]e−t/RC

with i(0+) fixed by the capacitor-voltage continuity rule vC​(0+)=vC​(0−) from AC Transients.

RL circuit with an AC source

Series RL: Z=R+jωL, ∣Z∣=R2+(ωL)2​, ϕ=tan−1(ωL/R), current lags:

iss​(t)=Im​sin(ωt−ϕ)

Time constant τ=L/R (same formula as RC/RL Transients and Time Constant). Complete response:

i(t)=Im​sin(ωt−ϕ)+Ae−Rt/L

At t=0+: i(0+)=−Im​sinϕ+A, so

A=i(0+)+Im​sinϕ

using iL​(0+)=iL​(0−) (inductor current continuity).

Worked pattern

v(t)=100sin(100t), R=10 Ω, L=0.1 H.

XL​=ωL=100(0.1)=10 Ω⇒Z=10+j10, ∣Z∣=14.142 Ω, ϕ=45°

Im​=14.142100​=7.071 A⟹iss​(t)=7.071sin(100t−45°)

τ=L/R=0.1/10=0.01 s, so the complete response has the form

i(t)=7.071sin(100t−45°)+Ae−100t

with A fixed once i(0+) is known from the pre-switch condition.
