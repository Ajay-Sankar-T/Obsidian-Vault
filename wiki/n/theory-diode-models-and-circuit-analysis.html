3. The "assume the state, then check" method

  1. Guess the state of every diode (ON or OFF).
  2. Replace each ON diode by its model (short, or battery Vγ​) and each OFF diode by an open circuit.
  3. Solve the resulting linear circuit (KVL/KCL, voltage divider, superposition).
  4. Check consistency: every ON diode must carry iD​≥0; every OFF diode must have vD​<Vγ​.
  5. If a check fails, change that diode's state and repeat.

With n diodes there are at most 2n guesses; in most problems the correct state is obvious (the diode whose anode is at the highest potential conducts).

6. Conventions

  • ω=314 rad/s means f=50 Hz (used in A-Q7 to A-Q9).
  • "Practical diode" means constant drop Vγ​=0.7 V unless a different value is stated.
  • Ripple voltage Vr​ is peak-to-peak unless stated; ripple factor RF is rms ripple over Vdc​.
