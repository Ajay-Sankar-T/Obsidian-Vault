Pattern recognition

You see Do
input on −, + grounded inverting: −Rf​/R1​
input on +, divider to − non-inverting: 1+Rf​/Rg​
several resistors into the − node inverting summer: add Vk​/Rk​
two inputs, four resistors difference amplifier; check R2​/R1​=R4​/R3​
capacitor in the feedback integrator: ramp, −RC1​∫Vi​dt
current source into the − node transimpedance: V=−Rf​i
"design a circuit for z=ax+by" pick the stage (weights of ± signs), then resistor ratios
finite A, Ri​, Ro​ given use the model, not the ideal rules

Exam-pattern hints

Questions are either analysis (apply the rules, KCL at v−​) or design (pick a topology that produces the sign and the weights, then resistor ratios; use standard values and 1 kΩ–1 MΩ). Always check polarity (inverting stages flip it) and units (Q8).
