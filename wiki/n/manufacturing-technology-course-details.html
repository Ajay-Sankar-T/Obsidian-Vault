Manufacturing Technology-I - ME2302
Approved Details
Description

• To give an overview on manufacturing, product quality and economy of production • To introduce various basic and advanced manufacturing technologies and elaborately explain process mechanics and tooling involved. • To impart a comprehensive understanding on the underlying science of each technology

Course Content

Introduction: Manufacturing, Product design, Material selection, Process route, General trends in manufacturing Mechanical behavior of materials and metal properties. Solidification and Molding Processes: Casting and moulding: Metal casting processes and equipment, Powder based processes, Heat treatment, Shaping of Polymer, ceramics and composites. Deformation Processes: Bulk forming and sheet metal forming. Subtractive Processes: Energy based classification, Mechanics of machining, Machining processes and operations, Tool wear and failure, Machinability, Cutting tool materials. Additive Processes: Additive manufacturing processes for shaping metals, polymers and ceramics. Joining Processes: Solid and liquid state joining processes; Brazing and soldering, Adhesive bonding. Product specific process selection Case Studies

TextBooks

• Kalpakjian and Schmid, Manufacturing processes for engineering materials (5th Edition)-Pearson India, 2014

Reference Books

• Mikell P. Groover, Fundamentals of Modern Manufacturing: Materials, Processes, and Systems.

Manufacturing Technology-II - ME3305
Approved Details
Description

• To explore the basic support functionalities needed in a machine to enable manufacturing process to shape materials. • To explore ways to inspect the geometric shape (form, fit, and finish) made by manufacturing processes.

Course Content

Rigid positioning of tools and parts: Tool and work holding devices, Jigs and fixtures. Movement of tools and parts: Overview of tool-work relative kinematics, Linear motion, Rotary motion, Mechanical synchronization of linear and rotary motion (machine kinematics), Programmable positioning and synchronization of linear and rotary motions (electric motor drives, CNC, programming of tool paths). Movement of parts between machines: Material transport systems, Part identification and data capture, Robotics. Manufacturing factory: CAD/CAM, CAPP; Process route modeling, Layout optimization, Economics of manufacturing, Case studies & industrial applications; Automation in manufacturing cells, assembly lines; Digital and smart manufacturing (Industry 4.0). Measurement: Definition of metrology, Measurement standards- geometrical dimensional and surface finish; Geometrical measurements – Form measurements: straightness, flatness, roundness, cylindricity; Dimensional measurements: linear and angular measurements with applications; Surface roughness measurement and evaluation; Form and finish measurement systems: Profile and form testers and CMM, GD&T: Geometrical and dimensional tolerances, limits and fits, Gauging: design and selection.
