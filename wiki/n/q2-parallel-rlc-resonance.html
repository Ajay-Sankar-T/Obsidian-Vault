EE1100 Tutorial 4 — Q2: Parallel RLC Resonance

A current source is applied to a parallel RLC arrangement: R=10Ω,L=1H,C=1μF.

1. Theory

  • Resonant Frequency: ω0​=LC​1​
  • Quality Factor (Parallel): Q=ω0​LR​=RLC​​
  • Bandwidth: BW=Qω0​​=RC1​

2. Solution

(a) Resonant Frequency

ω0​=1×1×10−6​1​=1000rad/s​

(b) Quality Factor

Q=1011×10−6​​=10×10−3=0.01​

(c) Bandwidth

BW=10×1×10−61​=100,000rad/s​

(d) Frequency Points Using ω1,2​=ω02​+(BW/2)2​∓BW/2: ω02​=106, (BW/2)2=(50,000)2=2.5×109. ω1,2​=106+2.5×109​∓50,000≈50010∓50,000.

  • ω1​=50010−50,000=10rad/s​
  • ω2​=50010+50,000=100,010rad/s​

(e) Voltage across Capacitor Signal i(t)=1sin(1000t). Since ω=1000=ω0​, the circuit is at resonance. At resonance, ∣Z∣ is maximum. For ideal parallel RLC, Z→∞, but with R, Z=R=10Ω. Vmax​=Imax​×R=1×10=10V​.

(f) Capacitor Current IC,max​=ω0​C1​Vmax​​=Vmax​ω0​C=10×1000×1×10−6=0.01A​. (Note: The answer provided in the prompt says 0.00707 A, which might imply the source is RMS or there's a different configuration, but based on the peak values given, 0.01 A is the direct calculation. If i(t)=1sin(1000t), Ipeak​=1).
