Absolute, Gauge and Vacuum Pressure

fluid-mechanicsfluid-staticspressure

Definitions

Pg​=Pabs​−Patm​​⟹Pabs​=Patm​+Pg​​

Pvac​=Patm​−Pabs​​⟹Pabs​=Patm​−Pvac​​

Standard atmospheric pressure: Patm​≈101.325 kPa​.

Gauge pressure is what most pressure gauges actually read (they're calibrated to zero at atmospheric pressure); absolute pressure is what appears in the ideal-gas law and in every derivation from Pressure and the Hydrostatic Equation — mixing the two is one of the most common sources of sign errors in this topic.
