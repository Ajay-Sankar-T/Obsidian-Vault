Bernoulli Differential Equations

Related: Linear Differential Equations

▶ A first order DE of the form

y′+P(x)y=Q(x)yn,n=0, n=1

is called a Bernoulli DE. (Note n=0 or n=1 corresponds to a linear ODE — see Linear Differential Equations.)

Reduction to Linear Form

Let v=y1−n, so y=v1−n1​. Then

y′=1−n1​v1−n1​−1v′=1−n1​v1−nn​v′

Substituting into the DE:

1−n1​v1−nn​v′+P(x)v1−n1​=Q(x)v1−nn​

Dividing by v1−nn​ (assuming nonzero):

1−n1​v′+P(x)v=Q(x)

⟹v′+(1−n)P(x)v=(1−n)Q(x)

This is a linear Lagrange first-order ODE in v — solve using the method in Linear Differential Equations, then recover y=v1−n1​.
