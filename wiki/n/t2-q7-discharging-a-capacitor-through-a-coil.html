T2-Q7: Discharging a Charged Capacitor through an RL Coil

EE1100tutorial-solutionDC-circuitstransientsRLC

Hub: Tutorial 2 - Transient Analysis of DC Circuits · Theory: Theory - Transients in RL, RC and RLC Circuits · Previous: T2-Q6 - RC Circuit with a Two-Position Switch · Next: T2-Q8 - Switch Opens in an RC Network

A 5 µF capacitor, initially charged to 10 V, is discharged suddenly through a coil of inductance 2 H and resistance 200 Ω. (a) Find the current i(t). (b) The additional resistance needed for critical damping. [Sheet: i(t)=0.01601e−50tsin312.25t A, Rext​=1064.9 Ω]

Tests: series RLC natural response, damping classification, critical resistance.

Given

C=5 μF with vC​(0)=10 V, L=2 H, R=200 Ω, switch closes at t=0, i(0)=0. (The figure on the sheet shows 10 µF, 100 Ω and 1 H, but the printed answers correspond to the text values; see the last section.)

Principle and why it applies

Source-free series RLC: Ldt2d2i​+Rdtdi​+Ci​=0. The type of response depends on α=R/2L against ω0​=1/LC​. The initial conditions are i(0)=0 (inductor) and Ldtdi​(0)=vC​(0)−Ri(0)=10 V (KVL at t=0+: the whole capacitor voltage appears across L).

Solution

α=2LR​=4200​=50 s−1,ω0​=LC​1​=2×5×10−6​1​=316.2 rad/s,

Rc​=2CL​​=25×10−62​​=1264.9 Ω  >R=200 Ω ⇒ underdamped.

ωd​=ω02​−α2​=100000−2500​=312.25 rad/s.

With i=e−αt(Acosωd​t+Bsinωd​t): i(0)=0⇒A=0; dtdi​(0)=Bωd​=LV0​​=5⇒B=312.255​=0.01601:

i(t)=0.01601e−50tsin(312.25t) A(=ωd​LV0​​e−αtsinωd​t).

(b) Critical damping. The total resistance must be Rc​=1264.9 Ω, so the additional series resistance is

Rext​=Rc​−R=1264.9−200=1064.9 Ω.

Result

i(t)=0.01601e−50tsin312.25t A,Rext​=1064.9 Ω​

Figure: left, the underdamped current inside its ±0.016e−50t envelope (peak about 12.6 mA at about 4.5 ms); right, the same circuit with three resistances: critical damping (green) is the fastest return to zero without crossing it; larger R is slower; smaller R rings.

Check

ζ=R/Rc​=0.158<1 ✓ underdamped. The first peak is where tanωd​t=ωd​/α, tp​=ωd​1​arctanαωd​​=4.5 ms and ip​=0.01601e−0.226sin(1.412)≈12.6 mA. Energy: 21​CV02​=250 μJ all ends up as heat in R.

Trap / alternative

  • Text versus figure. With the figure's values (C=10 μF, R=100 Ω, L=1 H) the decay and ringing frequencies are identical (α=50, ωd​=312.25) but the amplitude doubles, V0​/(ωd​L)=0.03203 A, and Rc​=632.5 Ω so Rext​=532.5 Ω. The sheet's printed answer is the text version, which is what is solved above.
  • "Additional" resistance means Rc​−R, not Rc​.
  • Don't forget the 1/ωd​ factor; writing V0​/L as the amplitude is the critical-damping form, not the underdamped one.
