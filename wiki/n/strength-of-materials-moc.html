Cross-domain synthesis

Buckling, resonance, energy methods, and stress/strain transformation all turn out to be the same handful of mathematical structures shared with EE1100 (RLC circuits) and Differential Equations. See Cross-Domain Synthesis for the eleven explicit connections. Those most relevant here:

  • Second Moments of Area — One Integral, Many Jobs — I, J, EI, GJ, buckling, centre of pressure
  • Fluids and Solids — Pressure, Stress and Constitutive Laws — pressure vessels, G vs μ
  • Conservation Laws and Flow Networks — equilibrium and compatibility as KCL and KVL
  • Gears, Transformers and Reflected Impedance — shaft power P=Tω through a gear train
