Special Functions - MOC

differential-equationsspecial-functions

Map of content for the Special Functions lectures of MA 2102 (Lecture-18 through Lecture-28). Builds on Series Solutions - MOC (power series and Frobenius method) and ends where the tutorial sheets (Assignments 5–7) continue. Part of Differential Equations - MOC.

    FR --> BE["Bessel's equation\n(regular singular point)"]

    LP --> ROD["Rodrigues formula"]
    LP --> GF["Generating function"]

Legendre (Lectures 18–22)

  1. Legendre Polynomials and Rodrigues' Formula — series solution, Pn​(1)=1, explicit sum, Rodrigues (two derivations) · L18–19
  2. Generating Function of Legendre Polynomials — (1−2xt+t2)−1/2=∑Pn​tn and its consequences · L19
  3. Inner Product Spaces and Orthogonal Functions — vector spaces, inner products, norms, orthonormal sets, L2 · L20
  4. Orthogonality and Norm of Legendre Polynomials — Sturm–Liouville proof; 2n+12​ by Rodrigues and by G · L20–21
  5. Legendre Series and Fourier-Legendre Expansions — coefficients, examples, convergence theorem, least squares · L21
  6. Legendre Recurrence Relations and Integrals — recurrences I–VI, Beltrami, Christoffel, integral evaluations · L22

Gamma and Bessel (Lectures 23–25)

  1. Gamma Function — factorial interpolation, Γ(21​), negative arguments · L23
  2. Bessel's Equation and Bessel Functions of the First Kind — Frobenius solution, Jν​, three cases · L23–24
  3. Bessel Functions of the Second Kind and General Solution — Yν​, integer-order series, J vs Y behaviour · L24
  4. Bessel Function Properties and Recurrence Relations — derivative identities, recurrences, spherical Bessel, integrals · L24–25
  5. Bessel Generating Function — e2x​(t−1/t)=∑Jn​tn · L25

Bessel transformations and Sturm–Liouville (Lectures 26–28)

  1. Reducing Equations to Bessel Form — the template y=xau(bxc), Airy as order 1/3 · L26
  2. Fourier-Bessel Series — orthogonality with weight x, coefficients, convergence · L27
  3. Sturm-Liouville Problems — self-adjoint form, Lagrange identity, orthogonality, eigenfunction expansions · L27–28
