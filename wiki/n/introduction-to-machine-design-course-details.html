Course Details

Introduction to Machine Design - ME3203
Approved Details
Description

To understand and implement principles of stress based design of simple mechanical elements

Course Content

Design of shafts keys and couplings: Shaft materials, shaft loads and stresses. Design of shafts for fully Reversed bending and steady torsion. Design of fluctuating bending and torsion loads. Key types and design Bearings: Sliding: Lubrication basics, choice of lubricants and bearing geometry for given loads Rolling element bearings: Types, uses and selection based on load ratings Design of transmission elements: Spur Gear: Loading and stresses, Lewis bending equation and design factors, Surface stress consideration, designing gear mesh. Modifications and extensions to helical spur and worm gears Design of springs: Stresses in helical compression and tension springs. Design in static and fatigue loading Design of bolts and fasteners: Nomenclature, Load analysis, Design under static and dynamic conditions, Joint stiffness factor, Bolts and Dowel pins in shear
