T7-Q5 — Secondary Terminal Voltage Under Load

EE1100transformertutorial-solutionvoltage-regulationload-voltage

Hub: Tutorial 7 on Transformer · Previous: T7-Q4 - Referred Impedances and Short-Circuit Voltage · Next: T7-Q6 - Equivalent Circuit on the LT Side

A 10 kVA, 2500/250 V transformer: r1​=4.8 Ω, x1​=11.2 Ω, r2​=0.048 Ω, x2​=0.112 Ω. With the primary held at 2500 V, find the secondary terminal voltage when (a) the LV side carries a load impedance 5+j3.5 Ω, (b) the transformer delivers rated current at 0.8 pf lagging. [(a) 242 V (b) 241.6 V]

Tests: voltage regulation, series equivalent impedance.

Given

a=10; V1​=2500 V so E2​=V1​/a=250 V (no-load secondary voltage). Rated LV current I2r​=10000/250=40 A.

Principle and why it applies

Refer everything to the LV side: Re2​=r2​+r1​/a2=0.048+0.048=0.096 Ω, Xe2​=0.112+0.112=0.224 Ω. The source (250 V) drives Ze2​ in series with the load, so the terminal voltage is the source minus the drop in Ze2​.

Solution

(a) Ztot​=(5+0.096)+j(3.5+0.224)=5.096+j3.724 Ω, ∣Ztot​∣=6.311 Ω, I2​=250/6.311=39.61 A.

V2​=I2​∣ZL​∣=39.61×52+3.52​=39.61×6.103=241.7 V.

(b) Rated current at 0.8 lag: I2​=40 A, ϕ=36.87∘. Drops: I2​Re2​=3.84 V, I2​Xe2​=8.96 V. With V2​ as reference,

2502=(V2​+3.84⋅0.8+8.96⋅0.6)2+(8.96⋅0.8−3.84⋅0.6)2=(V2​+8.448)2+4.8642

V2​=62500−23.7​−8.448=241.5 V.

Approximate (regulation) formula: V2​≈250−8.448=241.55 V.

Result

(a) V2​=241.7 V (≈242 V). (b) V2​=241.5 V (approximate method 241.6 V).

Check

%VR=250250−241.5​=3.4 %, i.e. re,pu​cosϕ+xe,pu​sinϕ with rpu​=25040⋅0.096​=1.54 %, xpu​=3.58 %: 1.23+2.15=3.38 % ✓.

Trap / alternative

Using the unreferred impedances, or dropping r1​ and x1​ (they are as large as r2​,x2​ once referred). Also, (b) specifies the current at rated value, not the load impedance.
