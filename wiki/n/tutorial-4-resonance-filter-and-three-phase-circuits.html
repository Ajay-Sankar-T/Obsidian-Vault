"maximum voltage across C" VC​=QV at resonance (Q1, Q3)
a current source on R‖L‖C parallel formulas: Q=R/ω0​L, BW=1/RC (Q2)

"attenuate f1​ by A dB" Q∣x−1/x∣=10A/10−1​ (Q8)

Exam-pattern hints

Resonance questions are marked on exact versus approximate band edges, peak versus rms, and on not mixing the series and parallel formulas. Three-phase questions are marked on the line/phase bookkeeping (which quantity is 3​ larger in star and in delta) and on using the load's impedance angle in cosϕ.

Sheet answers that differ from a direct calculation

  • Q6: upper half-power frequency printed 1818 rad/s; it must be ω1​+BW=1618 rad/s.
  • Q9: printed Q=2857.8 VAr; Ssin30∘=3300 VAr (the printed value is P/2).
  • Q10: the three phasors printed as "line currents" are the phase currents (8.8 A); the line currents are 15.24 A. The supply voltage (220 V) is implied, not stated.
  • Q2(f): 0.00707 A is the rms value of the 0.01 A peak capacitor current.
  • Q5, Q11: rounding only (7.95 vs 7.96 nF; 10.39 vs 10.40 Ω).
