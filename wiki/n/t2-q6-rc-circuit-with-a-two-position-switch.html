T2-Q6: RC Circuit with a Two-Position Switch

EE1100tutorial-solutionDC-circuitstransientsRC-circuit

Hub: Tutorial 2 - Transient Analysis of DC Circuits · Theory: Theory - Transients in RL, RC and RLC Circuits · Previous: T2-Q5 - RL Circuit with a Switch Opening · Next: T2-Q7 - Discharging a Capacitor through a Coil

The switch S is thrown from position 1 to 2 at t=0. Find vC​(t) and iC​(t) for t≥0. A 10 mA source with a 4 kΩ shunt, a 4 kΩ at position 2, and the branch C=10 μF in series with 6 kΩ. [Sheet: vC​=40e−10t V, iC​=−4e−10t mA]

Tests: first-order RC discharge; initial value from a DC steady state.

Given

Position 1 connects the capacitor branch (C in series with 6 kΩ) to node a, which carries the 10 mA source and a 4 kΩ shunt. Position 2 connects the capacitor branch to a separate 4 kΩ resistor returning to ground. vC​ is positive at the top plate, iC​ flows into it.

Principle and why it applies

The capacitor voltage is continuous across the switching instant, so vC​(0+) equals the steady-state value reached in position 1. After the switch moves the source is gone and the capacitor discharges through whatever resistance it now sees: a source-free RC circuit, vC​=vC​(0+)e−t/RC.

Diagram

Solution

Before (t<0, position 1, long time): DC steady state, the capacitor branch carries no current, so the 10 mA flows only through the 4 kΩ shunt:

Va​=10 mA×4 kΩ=40 V,vC​(0−)=Va​=40 V (no drop in 6 kΩ since i=0).

So vC​(0+)=40 V.

After (t>0, position 2): the capacitor discharges through 6 kΩ+4 kΩ=10 kΩ:

τ=RC=10×103×10×10−6=0.1 s.

vC​(t)=40e−t/0.1=40e−10t V.

iC​=CdtdvC​​=10×10−6×(−400)e−10t=−4e−10t mA.

Result

vC​(t)=40e−10t V,iC​(t)=−4e−10t mA(t≥0)​

Check

iC​(0+)=−vC​(0+)/10 kΩ=−40/104=−4 mA ✓. The negative sign says the current actually leaves the positive plate: the capacitor is discharging. Energy: initially 21​(10 μ)(40)2=8 mJ; dissipated in the 10 kΩ: ∫i2Rdt=16×10−6×104×20.1​=8 mJ ✓.

Trap / alternative

The 4 kΩ in the source network plays no part in the discharge (it is disconnected from the capacitor after the switch). A common error is to include it in R, or to use vC​(0)=10 mA ×(4∥4), forgetting that in position 1 the capacitor branch is connected only to node a.

Concepts used

  • RC time constant, continuity of vC​, DC steady state (Theory - Transients in RL, RC and RLC Circuits)
