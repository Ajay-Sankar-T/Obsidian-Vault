First order (Lectures 30–32)

  1. Partial Differential Equations - Definitions and Direct Integration — order, linear / semilinear / quasi-linear / nonlinear, arbitrary functions · L30
  2. Formation of PDEs - Eliminating Constants and Functions — why the general solution of a first-order PDE carries one arbitrary function · L31
  3. Lagrange's Method for Quasi-linear PDEs — characteristics, grouping, multipliers, the eigenvalue method · L31–32
  4. Cauchy Problem for First Order PDEs — fitting an integral surface to data on a curve; when it fails · L32
  5. Complete, General and Singular Integrals — Clairaut's equation and envelopes · L31

Second order and the three model equations (Lectures 33–37)

  1. Classification of Second Order PDEs — B2−4AC, hyperbolic / parabolic / elliptic, regions where the type changes · L33
  2. Canonical Forms of Second Order PDEs — characteristic coordinates, the three reduction recipes · L33
  3. D'Alembert's Solution of the Wave Equation — travelling waves, domain of dependence, reflection at a boundary · L33–34
  4. Separation of Variables — product solutions, eigenvalue problems, superposition · L34
  5. Wave Equation on a Finite Interval — vibrating string, normal modes, plucked string · L34
  6. Heat Equation - Separation of Variables — decay of modes, insulated and heated ends, steady-state shift · L34–35
  7. Laplace Equation on Rectangles and Strips — sinh/cosh in y, four-edge superposition, semi-infinite strip · L36
  8. Laplace Equation in Polar Coordinates — disk, annulus, sector, Poisson's formula · L37
