Variation of Parameters

Related: Undetermined Coefficients · General Solution Structure Theorem · Variation of Parameters - 3rd Order

Motivation

For y′′+P(x)y′+Q(x)y=R(x), Undetermined Coefficients only works for special forms of R(x). Variation of parameters works for any continuous R(x): tanx,secx,lnx,1/x, etc. It only requires knowing yc​=c1​y1​+c2​y2​.

Idea: replace the constants c1​,c2​ by unknown functions u1​(x),u2​(x), and seek yp​=u1​(x)y1​(x)+u2​(x)y2​(x).

Derivation

Let y1​,y2​ be a fundamental set of the homogeneous equation. Differentiating yp​=u1​y1​+u2​y2​:

yp′​=u1′​y1​+u1​y1′​+u2′​y2​+u2​y2′​

Impose the constraint u1′​y1​+u2′​y2​=0 (5) to avoid second derivatives of u1​,u2​ later, so yp′​=u1​y1′​+u2​y2′​. Differentiating again:

yp′′​=u1′​y1′​+u1​y1′′​+u2′​y2′​+u2​y2′′​

Substituting yp​,yp′​,yp′′​ into the ODE, the homogeneous parts vanish, leaving

u1′​y1′​+u2′​y2′​=R(x).(6)

(5) and (6) form a 2×2 linear system in u1′​,u2′​ whose determinant is the Wronskian W(y1​,y2​)=y1​y2′​−y2​y1′​=0. By Cramer's rule:

u1′​=W−y2​R(x)​,u2′​=Wy1​R(x)​.

Final Formulas

u1​(x)=−∫W(x)y2​(x)R(x)​dx,u2​(x)=∫W(x)y1​(x)R(x)​dx

yp​=y1​∫W−y2​R​dx+y2​∫Wy1​R​dx

y=c1​y1​+c2​y2​+yp​

No arbitrary constants are kept in u1​,u2​ — they're absorbed into yp​.

Worked Examples

y′′+y=tanx, −2π​<x<2π​: y1​=cosx,y2​=sinx, W=1.

u1′​=−sinxtanx=−cosxsin2x​=−(secx−cosx),u2′​=sinx

u2​=−cosx; u1​=−ln∣secx+tanx∣+sinx.

yp​=cosx(−ln∣secx+tanx∣+sinx)+sinx(−cosx)=−cosxln∣secx+tanx∣

GS: y=c1​cosx+c2​sinx−cosxln∣secx+tanx∣.

y′′−2y′+y=xex​, x>0: repeated root r=1; y1​=ex,y2​=xex, W=e2x.

u1′​=e2x−xex⋅(ex/x)​=−1⇒u1​=−x,u2′​=x1​⇒u2​=lnx

yp​=ex(−x)+xexlnx=xex(lnx−1)

GS: y=c1​ex+c2​xex+xex(lnx−1). (This RHS is not UC-friendly, so variation of parameters is essential here.)

y′′+4y=sec2x: y1​=cos2x,y2​=sin2x, W=2.

u1′​=−21​tan2x⇒u1​=41​ln∣cos2x∣,u2′​=21​⇒u2​=2x​

yp​=41​cos2xln∣cos2x∣+2x​sin2x

GS: y=c1​cos2x+c2​sin2x+41​cos2xln∣cos2x∣+2x​sin2x.
