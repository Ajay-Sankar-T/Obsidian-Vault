# Cardiovascular Biomechanics - Project Map

## Project purpose

Design experimental apparatuses that characterize arterial mechanics in two complementary specimen configurations:

1. an intact, pressurized arterial segment (radial expansion), and
2. a planar arterial coupon loaded in circumferential and longitudinal directions (biaxial tension).

## Important distinction

The radial-expansion apparatus retains the artery as a tube and measures pressure-driven diameter change. The biaxial tester uses a planar coupon and independently loads the circumferential and longitudinal axes. The two setups answer related but non-interchangeable questions.
