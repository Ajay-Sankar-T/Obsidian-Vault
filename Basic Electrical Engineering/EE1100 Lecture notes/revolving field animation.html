1 · Spatial MMF distribution

Each phase wave stays on its own spatial axis and pulsates. Their sum travels around the air gap.

Phase-coil axes and instantaneous MMF vectors

The coil magnetic axes are fixed at A: 0°, B: 120°, C: 240°, clockwise from the top. Each phase vector grows, falls to zero, then reverses along its own axis as its current changes sign.

These are instantaneous space vectors along coil magnetic axes, not time phasors. A′, B′ and C′ mark the opposite ends of those axes.

2 · Revolving field in the machine

Space angle α is measured clockwise from the top. The wave peak and the N end share the same angle.
