## Cross-domain links
- **Exponential growth** ↔ [[Applications of First Order ODEs]] and [[Exponential Decay - Time Constants, Damping and Spatial Relaxation]]: constant-rate growth $dy/dt=ky$; Moore's and Wright's laws are special cases, and the "deceptive" phase is the flat start of the curve.
- **Systems thinking** ↔ [[Conservation Laws and Flow Networks]] and [[Measurements, Instrumentation and Control - MOC]]: a system diagram with loops is a feedback system; stocks and flows are conserved quantities in a network.
- **Network effects (Metcalfe)** ↔ [[Conservation Laws and Flow Networks]]: value grows with connections, congestion and trust limit it.
- **Auctions and pricing** ↔ [[Assignment 9 - Sponsored Search Market (VCG and GSP)]]: VCG prices equal the externality a bidder imposes, an optimisation (assignment) problem.
- **Case studies** (Ather, Reva) ↔ [[Mechanical Engineering Curriculum - MOC]]: the engineering behind them is the thermal, electrical and machine-design courses.
